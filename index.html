About Me

I am Abdirahman Yusuf Ahmed, a technology-oriented individual born in Hargeisa, Somaliland. My fascination with computers began early in life, reaching an essential milestone in 2015 when our household acquired its first laptop. That singular moment transformed a natural curiosity into a dedicated technological path.

Rather than waiting for traditional classroom courses, I leveraged YouTube and autonomous self-directed learning to build practical skills across graphic design, video editing, and digital media production. This foundation helped me transition into professional marketing operations and establish Hiigsi Technologies as an entrepreneurial venture.

Today, I am deepening my theoretical and engineering competencies as a Software Engineering student at Alhuda University, driven by the belief that software is the most transformative instrument for our society's future.
